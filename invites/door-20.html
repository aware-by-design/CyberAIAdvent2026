Door 20: A safe word for the family
DO: Agree a family safe word for emergency calls and messages.
DON’T: Send money on an urgent call from a “relative” or VIP - hang up and call back on a known number.
Lesson learnt (A cloned minister, Feb 2025): Scammers cloned the voice of Defence Minister Guido Crosetto to ask business leaders for money. At least one paid about €1 million before police froze the funds.
